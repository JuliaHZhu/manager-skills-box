# Anti-Involution Diagnoser

Diagnose organizational involution (内卷) and internal waste (内耗) using Huawei's 30-type involution checklist and 10-waste framework. Use when organizational energy is consumed by internal competition, meaningless complexity, and bureaucratic theater rather than value creation.

## When to Use

Activate when the user describes:
- 内卷 / involution / 内耗 / internal friction
- 部门墙 / 大公司病 / big company disease
- 无意义加班 / 精益求精但没价值 / meaningless overtime
- 胶片文化 / PPT文化 / presentation theater
- 管控过度 / 监控指标造假 / excessive control with gaming
- 马屁文化 / 官僚主义 / sycophancy / bureaucracy
- 组织诊断 / organizational health check
- 效率低下 / 人人很忙但没结果 / busy but no output

Do NOT activate for: individual procrastination, external market competition, or normal organizational growing pains in startups.

## Core Concepts

### What is Involution (内卷)?

**Definition**: When a development stage reaches a determined form but stagnates — unable to transform to a higher mode. Social resources cannot meet all needs; people compete through mutual crushing and internal consumption.

**通俗说**: Excessive competition in a domain causing mutual erosion and internal waste.

### Huawei's 30 Types of Involution (Ch.30)

**Key categories**:

1. **Structural involution**:
   - 部门墙是内卷 (#1)
   - 权利和责任割裂 (#6 in Ch.32)
   - 集权而低效的组织设计 (#7)

2. **Behavioral involution**:
   - 故意为合作伙伴制造障碍 (#2)
   - 上班时间发帖划水 (#4)
   - 通过越来越严重加班竞争绩效 (#10)
   - 电话会议能解决的非要飞来飞去 (#14)

3. **Process/formalism involution**:
   - 无意义的精益求精 (#1 of 7 typical)
   - 将简单问题复杂化 (#2)
   - 为了免责被动应付 (#3)
   - 胶片文化 (#8) — 领导说减少胶片，结果照旧
   - 无休止的总结，不相信前人结论 (#29)
   - 跟毫不相干的人员拉通对齐 (#30)

4. **Assessment involution**:
   - 考试不给翻书还不告诉错题 (#5)
   - 大比武考试 (#11)
   - 每月扫雷行动必须发现问题 (#12)
   - 没完没了的考试邮件 (#13)
   - 活干完了还得找亮点 (#24)

5. **Innovation/ideological involution**:
   - 预研可信就是最大内卷 (#6)
   - 言必称IBM/爱立信 (#9 in Ch.32 / #18 in 30-types)
   - 企图把复杂问题简单化教条 (#26)
   - 站在道德制高点说正确废话 (#28)

### Diagnostic Approach

**Wrong way**: Monitor easily-gamed metrics (meetings reduced, test pass rates, quality delays)
- Result: Gaming — e.g., "no more failed tests" means tests are rigged

**Right way**: Organizational health examination (组织级健康审视)
- Examine management climate and atmosphere
- Platform-based employee activation, not control-based
- Ask: Are people creating value or protecting themselves?

## Operational Workflow

When user describes organizational dysfunction:

### Step 1: Symptom Classification
Map symptoms to categories:
- **Structural**: Department walls, power/responsibility split, centralization
- **Behavioral**: Gaming, sycophancy, overtime competition
- **Formalism**: Presentations, meaningless reviews, excessive reporting
- **Assessment**: Exam culture, finding亮点 after work done
- **Ideological**: Copying others, slogans without substance, arrogance

### Step 2: Severity Scoring
For each of the 30 types / 10 wastes present:
- **Mild**: Occasional, not systemic
- **Moderate**: Common, accepted as "how things are"
- **Severe**: Dominant mode of operation; drives behavior

### Step 3: Root Cause Analysis
Ask five whys to identify whether root cause is:
- **Structure** (org design creates conflict)
- **Incentive** (metrics reward wrong behavior)
- **Culture** (values not translated to behavior)
- **Leadership** (top behavior cascades down)

### Step 4: Intervention Design

**For structural**: Redesign process, matrix, responsibility centers (use org-synergy-architect)
**For incentive**: Separate selection from evaluation; reduce KPI count; add backward event review (use result-orientation-guardian)
**For culture**: Translate values to specific behaviors per scenario; link to recognition (use simple-org-culture-builder)
**For leadership**: Require skip-level feedback; 360-degree with consequences

### Step 5: Monitor Health, Not Metrics
- Shift from "metric compliance" to "climate assessment"
- Use anonymous pulse surveys on psychological safety
- Track cross-department collaboration frequency (network analysis)
- Measure time spent on value-creating vs protective activities

## Key Distinctions

**Involution vs Innovation**:
- Involution: Same problem, endless digging; zero-sum internal competition
- Innovation: New solutions; positive-sum value creation

**Control vs Activation**:
- Control: Assume people will cheat; add monitoring
- Activation: Assume people want to achieve; build platform and clarity

**Metric Gaming vs Real Improvement**:
- Gaming: Optimize metric definition to look good
- Real: Change behavior that produces genuine value

## Output Format

When activated, provide:
1. **Involution Inventory**: Which of 30 types / 10 wastes are present
2. **Severity Heat Map**: Mild/Moderate/Severe classification
3. **Root Cause Diagnosis**: Structure, incentive, culture, or leadership
4. **Intervention Roadmap**: Prioritized fixes with skill cross-references
5. **Health Dashboard**: How to monitor climate instead of gaming metrics

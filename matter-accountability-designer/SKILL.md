# matter-accountability-designer

## Metadata

- **Source**: Huawei Management Handbook (5th Ed) — Business Management, Chapters 7.7, 28
- **Domain**: Organizational accountability / Process culture
- **Confidence**: High (derived from documented Huawei practice)
- **Revision**: 1.0

## When to Activate

Use when the user needs to build a "matter accountability" (对事负责) system, reduce excessive upward reporting, eliminate "relationship-first" internal politics, or design processes where people are responsible to the task rather than to the boss. Also triggers when the user complains that everything requires the boss's signature, or that decisions are made to please superiors rather than achieve results.

**Key triggers**: 对事负责 / 对人负责 / matter accountability / 责任结果导向 / result-oriented responsibility / 事事请示 / 减少审批 / 流程负责 / 不是对人负责 / accountability by matter / 目标导向

**Not for**: individual performance coaching without systemic redesign, or compliance/audit scenarios where hierarchical reporting is legally required.

## Core Methodology

任正非: "对事负责制是一种扩张体系，只对目标负责；对人负责制是一种收敛体系，这体系的弊端就是拉关系，走投机路线。"

### Principle 1: Matter Accountability = Expansion; Person Accountability = Contraction

| Dimension | Matter Accountability (对事负责) | Person Accountability (对人负责) |
|-----------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Orientation | Target / result | Boss / relationship |
| System type | Expansion (growth-oriented) | Contraction (risk-averse) |
| Behavior | Proactive, autonomous | Reactive,请示-driven |
| Culture | Merit-based, open | Politics-based, guarded |
| Speed | Fast (rules known, no need to ask) | Slow (wait for approval) |
| Failure mode | Occasional misalignment | Bureaucratic paralysis |

### Principle 2: Build the Matter-Accountability Infrastructure

1. **Clear rules and precedents** (规定和惯例):
   - If something is already regulated or has become惯例, employees must execute without asking.
   - 任正非: "已经有规定，或者成为惯例的东西，不必请示，应快速让它通过去。"
   - Every请示 that could have been avoided is organizational waste.

2. **Process ownership, not boss ownership**:
   - The process is the boss. Employees are accountable to the process and its output standards.
   - Decisions are made at the流程节点 (process node) by the person closest to the information, not by the highest-ranking available manager.

3. **Simplify confirmation layers**:
   - Remove unnecessary approval steps. Each additional签名 adds delay without proportional value.
   - 任正非: "要简化不必要确认的东西，要减少在管理中不必要、不重要的环节，否则公司怎么能高效运行呢？"

### Principle 3: Responsibility-Result Orientation (责任结果导向)

1. **Result, not motion**:
   - Evaluate based on actual results delivered, not on activities performed or hours worked.
   - "Fake action" (假动作) — busywork that produces no value — must be identified and eliminated.

2. **Decompose crisis and contradiction layer by layer**:
   - Top-level goals are broken into departmental targets, then team targets, then individual targets.
   - Each layer owns its分解目标 and is judged on it.

3. **考评机制 aligned with matter accountability**:
   - Performance reviews assess: Did you meet the target? Did you follow the process? Did you improve the process?
   - Not: Did the boss like you? Did you attend enough meetings?

### Principle 4: Eliminate "Person-First" Behaviors

| Person-First Behavior | Matter-First Replacement |
|-----------------------|--------------------------|
| "Is this what the leader wants?" | "Is this what the target requires?" |
| "Let me check with my boss first." | "The rule says X; I will do X and report the result." |
| Building relationships to secure approvals | Building competence to deliver results |
| 联合签名 to pressure decisions | Individual专业意见 submitted through proper channels |
| Email to boss for every decision | Execute within authority; escalate exceptions only |

### Principle 5: Protect and Redirect

1. **Protect employees who act matter-first**:
   - If an employee follows the process and the result is suboptimal due to external factors, protect them from blame.
   - If an employee bypasses process to please a boss and causes damage, hold them accountable.

2. **Redirect请示 culture**:
   - Track请示 volume by department. Rising请示 rates indicate unclear rules or fear culture.
   - Train managers to say: "What does the process say?" instead of "What do I think?"

3. **Lead by example**:
   - Senior leaders must model matter-accountability: they should be judged by organizational results, not by how many decisions they personally made.

## STOP Checkpoints

- **STOP if** a process still requires the boss's signature for routine steps that could be rule-based.
- **STOP if** performance evaluations include "relationship with manager" as a formal or informal criterion.
- **STOP if** employees are punished for making decisions within their authority when the outcome is unfavorable due to factors beyond their control.
- **STOP if** the organization adds approval layers in response to a single mistake (overreaction creates bureaucracy).

## Anti-Patterns (Blacklist)

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails | Correct Practice |
|--------------|--------------|------------------|
| Culture of请示 | Wastes leader time; slows execution | Clear authority boundaries; execute first |
| Boss-dependent decision-making | Decisions optimize for politics, not results | Process-dependent decision-making |
| Relationship-based promotion | Incentivizes internal公关 over value creation | Result-based advancement |
| Collective signatures | Pressure tactics replace merit | Individual专业意见 through proper channels |
| Adding approvers after every error | Bureaucracy grows; accountability diffuses | Fix the process, don't add signatures |

# Strategic Reserve Operator

Operate a strategic reserve / heavy brigade system that trains, rotates, and deploys talent to capture strategic opportunity windows and prevent organizational sclerosis.

## When to Use

When the user needs to:
- Build a strategic reserve or "heavy brigade" (重装旅) to seize opportunity windows
- Design a "train-fight-return" cycle that keeps talent fluid and battle-ready
- Prevent organizational hardening by maintaining continuous rotation and entropy reduction
- Allocate costs and risks between the reserve and frontline projects
- Turn project wins/losses into organizational learning via case writing and knowledge inheritance

## Core Concepts

**Three Strategic Reserves**: Heavy Brigade (solution sales), Major Project Department (delivery), Project Management Resource Pool (PM talent).

**Snowball Mechanism**: Talent flows into the reserve, gets trained, fights on real projects, then returns — the reserve grows larger with each cycle like a rolling snowball.

**Cost Mechanism**: Win = costs absorbed by the project; Lose = reserve pays its own dry-run costs. This creates "raise glass in victory, fight to the death in defeat" culture.

**Selection Gates**: Top 25% performers + structural reform redeployees + crisis-evacuated overseas staff. Two-gate entry: business unit nomination + reserve interview.

**Four-Phase Training**: Online self-study + certification → face-to-face drill and sandbox → real project combat (2–6 months) → defense and certification before exit.

## Operating Procedure

### Step 1: Define Reserve Mission

Clarify which of the three strategic jobs the reserve serves:
1. **Capability transformer** — retool existing talent for new business models
2. **Opportunity window snatcher** — when explosive growth appears, rapidly staff new battles
3. **Organizational activator** — circulate blood, break silos, prevent super-stable inertia

Set the reserve’s scope (solution domain, geography, function) and its reporting line.

### Step 2: Design Entry Gates

Establish dual-gate entry:
- **Gate A (Nomination)**: Business units nominate based on performance rank (top 25%) or structural reform needs. No volunteer-only; must be sponsored.
- **Gate B (Assessment)**: Reserve conducts capability interview and scenario test. Pass = admission.

Document three entry streams:
- Annual top performers
- Structural-reform redeployees
- Crisis-evacuated staff from high-risk regions

### Step 3: Build Train-Fight-Integrate Loop

Design the four-phase cycle:

| Phase | Duration | Activity | Output |
|-------|----------|----------|--------|
| Self-study | 2–4 weeks | Online courses + exams on tools, templates, codes | Certification pass |
| Drill | 1–2 weeks | Case研讨, sandbox simulation, war-game演练 | Skill demonstration |
| Combat | 2–6 months | Embedded in real projects with reserve identity | Project result + observer report |
| Defense | 1 week | Presentation of combat results, peer/leader review | Exit rating (excellent / pass / fail) |

**Critical rule**: Training scenarios must use *exact* templates, codes, identifiers, and formats that the real battlefield uses. No simplified teaching versions.

### Step 4: Establish Cost and Incentive Rules

- **Winning project**: Project budget covers reserve member costs; reserve earns reputation credits.
- **Losing project**: Reserve pays its own dry-run costs (空载费用); members absorb the loss as training tuition.
- **Individual incentive**: Reserve completion is a prerequisite for promotion to key posts. High exit rating = fast-track placement.
- **Collective incentive**: Reserve culture slogan — "胜则举杯相庆，败则拼死相救" (raise glass in victory, fight to the death in defeat).

### Step 5: Deploy and Re-integrate

After defense, route members based on exit rating:
- **Excellent** → promoted to next-level key岗位 or assigned to strategic new markets
- **Pass** → returned to original unit with enhanced capabilities; unit must accept re-integration
- **Fail** → returned to original unit with development advice; reserve records failure pattern for curriculum improvement

Maintain alumni network: former reserve members are expected to mentor next cohort and contribute new cases.

### Step 6: Institutionalize Case Writing

Require every departing member to produce:
- One case write-up (success or failure) with personal authorship credit
- One lessons-learned summary tied to specific battle scenes

Cases become internal textbooks. Project summaries carry personal copyright and signature —计入光荣史 as honor and motivation.

## Anti-Patterns

- **Training without fighting**: Classroom-only reserve turns into a vacation camp or bureaucracy.
- **Fighting without training**: Throwing unprepared people onto projects destroys both project and morale.
- **Permanent reserve population**: Members must flow *through*, not stay in the reserve forever.
- **Win-only accounting**: If the reserve never pays for losses, it has no skin in the game and no incentive to improve.
- **Ignoring rotation after return**: Without re-integration support, returning members face envy or isolation.

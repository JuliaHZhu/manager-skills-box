# Result Orientation Guardian

Implement result-oriented management with Huawei's dual approach: forward KPI考核 plus backward event-based考事 (examining deeds). Covers cascading crisis decomposition, layered assessment, and the three-pillar framework of Customer-Process-Performance.

## When to Use

Activate when the user discusses:
- 责任结果导向 / result-oriented / outcome-based management
- 正向考核 / 逆向考事 / forward assessment + backward event review
- 危机分解 / 矛盾层层分解 / cascading pressure
- 分层考核 / layered assessment
- 客户流程绩效 / customer-process-performance
- KPI太多 / 过程考核绑架结果 / 干部只看KPI不看结果
- 干部管理四象限 / cadre management quadrants

Do NOT activate for: pure OKR setting, individual productivity hacks, or performance reviews in stable environments without cadre management context.

## Core Concepts

### 1. Result Orientation vs Process Orientation

**The Fable** (from Huawei materials):
- Idealist pig: Advanced KPI system, century-long strategy, everyone works hard in the system
- Result-oriented pig: Sales and customer system, analyzes needs, rewards contributors, everyone acts fast
- **Outcome**: Result-oriented pig's company performs 2x better

**Lesson**: Scientific management without good results is meaningless. Good motivation without good effect is insufficient.

**任正非**: "不能带来好结果的素质，不能称之为素质" — Capability that doesn't produce results is not capability.

### 2. Three-Layer Decomposition

**Customer (灵魂/魂)**:
- "产品发展的路标是客户需求导向"
- Customer is the opium — obsession with customer satisfaction
- All work starts and ends with customer needs
- Customer doesn't care about your internal structure; only cares about result and price

**Process (责任止于流程)**:
- Process weaves the organizational net: interlinked, responsive
- Like a dragon: head (Marketing) pursues customer needs; body follows while internal relationships remain stable
- Process enables "responsibility stops at process" — no buck-passing
- Bad process: Customer must travel to downstream to board upstream train

**Performance (生存依靠绩效)**:
- Performance = profit margin. Enterprise exists to create value.
- No performance = no blood circulation = organizational death
- International marketing is a cash-flow war; whoever keeps cash wins
- 任正非 on Osaka castle: "Cash flow is the deep well — defend it, and the castle holds"

### 3. Crisis Decomposition (危机层层分解)

**Problem**: Frontline burns with urgency; back office moves slowly. Top-level concerns dissipate before reaching bottom.

**Huawei solution**: Decompose crisis and contradiction to every level.
- 任正非 (1999): "KPI system must decompose crisis and contradiction layer by layer. Any department or cadre with no crisis can be removed."

**Implementation**:
- Separate administrative and business management
- Build multi-layer, multi-level, multi-specialty responsibility centers
- Delegate authority to those closest to decisions
- Layered assessment by level and category

**Layered assessment** (《华为基本法》):
- Two-level assessment: direct superior (1st), superior's superior (2nd)
- Three layers: high/middle/low with different standards
- Cross-department categories: e.g., all secretaries use same assessment

### 4. Forward Assessment + Backward Event Review

**Forward (正向考核)**:
- PBC: Win (results), Executive (key measures), Team (collaboration)
- Top-down decomposition: company → department → individual
- KPI should be few, not many. 任正非 opposes behavior-heavy KPIs.

**Backward (逆向考事)**:
- Examine every error to find root cause
- Discover good cadres through failure analysis
- 任正非: "Avoid absolute, metaphysical assessment"

**Key distinction**: Process behavior is for **cadre selection**, not for **performance evaluation**.
- 任正非 (2013): "关键过程行为考核是用来选拔干部的，人家事都做成了，过程为什么要成为评奖金的指标呢？"

### 5. Cadre Management Quadrant

| | High Results | Low Results |
|---|---|---|
| **High Quality** | Promote | Demote to基层 |
| **Low Quality** | Train/Develop | Remove |

**Rule**: Those with high quality but poor result orientation cannot be promoted to management. They can only do individual contributor work.

## Operational Workflow

When user asks about result-oriented management implementation:

### Step 1: Audit Current KPI System
- Count number of KPIs per role (Huawei advocates few)
- Check if KPI completion correlates with actual business results
- Identify KPI-driven gaming (e.g., delaying GA to "show quality focus")

### Step 2: Implement Crisis Decomposition
- Map company-critical metrics to each department
- Ensure every level feels market pressure
- Remove cadres in departments with "no crisis"

### Step 3: Design Layered Assessment
- Define high/middle/low layers with different standards
- Break department barriers for same-role assessment
- Two-level validation (direct + skip-level)

### Step 4: Separate Selection from Evaluation
- Use forward KPI for performance evaluation (bonus, salary)
- Use backward event review and behavior observation for cadre selection/promotion
- Don't mix them: "不要在一个东西上承载太多内容，让人都变成小人了"

### Step 5: Validate Customer-Process-Performance Chain
- Does every role connect to customer value?
- Does process enable rather than block customer service?
- Does performance metric reflect sustainable value creation?

## Key Distinctions

**Behavior for Selection vs Results for Evaluation**:
- Most companies: Evaluate behavior to pay bonus (creates gaming)
- Huawei: Evaluate results to pay bonus; observe behavior to select cadres

**Crisis Decomposition vs Micromanagement**:
- Decomposition: Everyone feels market pressure autonomously
- Micromanagement: Top dictates every action

**Layered Assessment vs Same Standard**:
- Layered: Different expectations for different levels
- Same standard: Unrealistic and unfair

## Output Format

When activated, provide:
1. **KPI Audit**: Current KPI count and result correlation analysis
2. **Cascading Map**: How company crisis metrics decompose to each level
3. **Assessment Framework**: Layered evaluation structure
4. **Separation Charter**: What drives evaluation (results) vs selection (behavior)
5. **Three-Pillar Check**: Customer alignment, process support, performance sustainability

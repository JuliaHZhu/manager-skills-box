# Simple Org Culture Builder

Build a simple, resilient organization where capability resides in systems rather than individuals, using Huawei's three-mechanism framework (authority/money/evaluation) and culture-to-behavior translation. Use when organizational complexity suffocates effectiveness or culture remains slogans on walls.

## When to Use

Activate when the user discusses:
- 简单组织 / simple organization / 简单驱逐复杂
- 建制 / 能力建在组织上 / capability in organization not individuals
- 分权机制 / 分钱机制 / 评价机制 / authority/money/evaluation mechanisms
- 价值观落地 / values to behavior / 文化落地
- 契约精神 / contract-based organization
- 江湖化 / 家文化 / 熟人模式 / clique culture / family business culture
- 胜任是伪命题 / 建制才是成功保证
- 管理到位 / management execution
- 歪瓜裂枣 / tolerating eccentric talent
- 狼狈组织 / wolf-and-bie team pairing

Do NOT activate for: individual coaching, simple team-building activities, or compensation design without organizational context.

## Core Concepts

### 1. Capability in Organization, Not Individuals (能力建在组织上)

**"胜任是个伪命题"**: You cannot prove capability before someone works. Only the organization can prove whether someone is competent through its mechanisms.

**Huawei's approach**:
- Lower hiring bar; raise organizational bar
- Work decomposition structure (WBS): Break work into executable activities anyone can do
- 合弄制 (holacracy-like teaming): Individual + organization system = completed work
- **Rule**: If a role can't rotate, the organization is not safely built

**Three-month rule**: If business problem occurs within 3 months of handover,前任 bears primary responsibility; after 3 months,现任 bears it. This forces complete knowledge transfer.

**Knowledge replication**: 
- Human capital增值 = knowledge复制 speed
- At sufficient talent density, knowledge grows geometrically
- 3G example: 5000 people储备; even though China 3G was delayed, overseas commercial contracts built experience for domestic comeback

### 2. Three Mechanisms (分权、分钱、评价)

**Mechanism 1: Authority (分权机制)**
- Divide work to make it simple (分工让工作简单)
- Limit human weakness through division: nomination, review, decision, impeachment powers separated
- 干部任命: 三权分立 (three-power separation) prevents personal fiefdoms

**Mechanism 2: Money (分钱机制)**
- Built on contribution (建立在贡献之上)
- Performance, capability, values as basis
- Ultimately guides to contribution

**Mechanism 3: Evaluation (评价机制)**
- Evaluates performance, capability, values
- **Purpose**: Ensure authority is controlled, money is earned
- Without evaluation,分权 and分钱 fail

**Management到位 means**: 分好权, 分好钱, 评到位, 管到位

### 3. Simple Organization

**Huawei's cultural tattoo**:
- Customer is opium
- Extreme result and target orientation
- Argue fiercely inside, execute collectively outside
- Military-grade execution
- Internally cynical; externally proud to be Huawei

**Management最高境界 = 简单 (Simple)**:
- Simple people + simple organization
- 任正非: "我就是阿甘" — Simple persistence beats clever complexity
- 简单驱逐复杂: Those who play tricks don't占便宜; contributors with坦荡做人做事脱颖而出

**Warning**: When Huawei people become complex, and organization becomes layered (叠床架屋), decline begins.

### 4. Culture-to-Behavior Translation

**Problem**: Most companies' values are空洞 or随意.
- Examples of bad values: "开拓创新, 开放进取, 以德为先" — too big, too vague

**Huawei's values** (闭环利益-观念体系):
- 以客户为中心 → 价值创造来源
- 以奋斗者为本 → 价值评价标准
- 长期艰苦奋斗 → 价值分配原则

**Three-in-one闭环**: Where does money come from? Who creates it? How to distribute? Why?

**Behavioral translation method**:
- Decompose business process into scenarios
- For each scenario, define how values manifest in specific behaviors
- Example — Teamwork in IPD:
  - Good behavior: Send competent speaker who contributes, regardless who initiates meeting
  - Bad behavior: Send inappropriate person, arrive late, don't engage

**Most companies fail**: They evaluate values via 360-degree scores or overtime hours. Huawei evaluates specific behaviors in specific contexts.

### 5. Contract Over Loyalty (契约高于忠诚)

**Huawei's evolution**:
- Early years: "用人不疑" — trust-based, family-like
- ~1996-2002: Betrayals and山头主义; 任正非 depression
- 2002+: "用人要疑" — system-dependent, not person-dependent
- 2008+: "从不信任管理走向信任管理" — spiral ascent, not regression

**Key rules**:
- 换防制: Manager rotates every 3 years, max 5 years in one department
- No circles, no cliques, no结盟
- 任正非 has few friends inside Huawei — deliberate
- 忠诚 = loyalty to事业 and公司, not to person (including任正非)

**契约 essence**: Organization-employee relationship is transaction-based.
- If employee can't contribute → adjust or remove
- If company no longer fits employee → clean separation
- Both sides honor契约

**Avoid Chinese management黑洞**: 
- 江湖化 (江湖文化): "自己人" vs outsiders; trust based on relationship not principle
- 家文化: Family/clan business with血缘/准血缘 loyalty
- 结果: 南桔北枳 — heroism turns to枭雄ism without values and systems

### 6. Tolerating Eccentric Talent (歪瓜裂枣)

**任正非**: "我们选的是战士，而非完美的苍蝇"
- 战士: 冲锋陷阵, 有缺点, 伤痕累累
- 苍蝇: 嗡嗡叫, 传播细菌, 盯着战士缺点

**Principle**: Don't弥补缺点; make strengths longer.
- 110m hurdler hires coach to improve hurdling, not long jump
- Match person to岗位要求; don't require完人

**狼狈组织计划**:
- 狼: 嗅觉灵敏, 团队协作, 坚韧不拔 (frontline attacker)
- 狈: 精明, 策划能力强, 前腿短不能独立进攻 (back office supporter)
- 狈抱狼后腿, 操控进攻方向
- Every department needs both

**Layered talent requirements**:
- 基层: 执行力 (execution)
- 中层: 理解力 (comprehension)
- 高层: 决断力 (decision)

### 7. Organization Management Six Elements (组织管理六大要素)

1. **Build brick by brick**: Organization strength is the试金石; no shortcuts
2. **Strong内生力量**: Diamond-hard, pure, cohesive; but also open to new blood
3. **Contract-based transaction**: Even "子弟兵" are there for value exchange; no free loyalty
4. **Universal values for openness**: Values must be based on human nature to attract global talent
5. **Organization-person interaction**: Ambition turns to野心 without values and systems
6. **Western mainstream人性假设**: People are unreliable; systems are more reliable, though imperfect

## Operational Workflow

When user wants to build or transform organizational culture:

### Step 1: Organizational Capability Audit
- Can roles rotate without disruption? (If no → capability not in org)
- Is knowledge replication speed sufficient?
- What's the handover quality?

### Step 2: Three-Mechanism Check
- **Authority**: Is work divided simply? Are nomination/review/decision separated?
- **Money**: Is distribution tied to contribution?
- **Evaluation**: Does evaluation protect the other two mechanisms?

### Step 3: Values Behavioral Translation
- Pick 3-5 core values (not slogans)
- For each business scenario (meeting, decision, conflict, customer interaction), define specific behaviors
- Link behaviors to recognition and consequence

### Step 4: Anti-Clique Structure
- Implement rotation system (3-year rule)
- Separate powers in key decisions
- CEO deliberately avoids building personal circles

### Step 5: Tolerance Design
- Identify which "flaws" are acceptable for high performers
- Build complementary teams (wolf + bie pairing)
- Match strengths to岗位要求; don't punish for irrelevant weaknesses

### Step 6: Simplicity Metrics
- Count management layers
- Count approval steps for routine decisions
- Measure time spent on value creation vs internal coordination
- Target: Reduce complexity by 20% annually

## Key Distinctions

**Organization capability vs Individual heroism**:
- Heroism: Depend on star performers; fragile
- Organization: System produces results regardless of who plays

**Values as behavior vs Values as slogan**:
- Slogan: Written on wall, forgotten in action
- Behavior: Defined per scenario, measured, rewarded

**Contract vs Loyalty**:
- Loyalty: Emotional, personal, fragile when tested
- Contract: Clear, mutual, enforceable, scalable

**Simple vs Simplistic**:
- Simple: Elegant design, easy to execute, hard to game
- Simplistic: Ignoring complexity; naive solutions

## Output Format

When activated, provide:
1. **Capability Map**: Where does capability reside — individuals or systems?
2. **Three-Mechanism Scorecard**: Authority, money, evaluation health check
3. **Values Behavior Matrix**: Scenarios × expected behaviors
4. **Anti-Clique Protocol**: Rotation and power-separation design
5. **Simplicity Dashboard**: Complexity metrics and reduction targets

# Elite + Soldier + Civilian Architect

Design a three-tier talent structure inspired by Huawei's "elite + soldier + civilian" (精英+精兵+职员) model. Covers selection standards, wolf-and-beaver partnerships, zigzag rotation, training-battle integration, three-power-separation appointment, and flow mechanisms to sustain organizational vitality.

## When to Use

- When designing a talent system that needs both heroic leaders and stable operators
- When building leadership pipelines for complex, multi-domain businesses
- When frontline teams need both strategic autonomy and disciplined support
- When you want to prevent rigidity in a growing workforce while maintaining quality

## How It Works

### Tier 1: Elite & Soldier Selection (精英和精兵选拔)

**Three mandatory criteria** (all must be met):
1. **Quality** (素质): Align with core values; possess self-critique capability; pass behavioral event interviews (e.g., willingness to go to hardship posts, taking responsibility under pressure)
2. **Performance** (绩效): Necessary condition and watershed. Stars come from star teams; continuous underperformance → removal
3. **Experience** (经验): Verified capability through results. "Fierce generals arise from the ranks, ministers from the provinces" (猛将必发于卒伍，宰相必取于州郡)

**Role-specific standards**:
- **Senior leaders**: Decisiveness + interpersonal connectivity. Set direction, manage paradoxes, control pace.
- **Middle managers**: Comprehension. Translate strategy into execution, transmit pressure downward as vitality.
- **Frontline leaders**: Execution. Deliver results tenaciously.
- **Wolf-and-beaver pairing** (狼狈合作):
  - **Wolf (正职/作战主官)**: Focuses solely on victory; aggressive, strategic, self-sacrificing; disqualify if overly gentle or micromanaging
  - **Beaver (副职/平台主官)**: Provides precise support; manages operations, defends gains; disqualify if careless or laissez-faire

**Three-power-separation appointment process** (三权分立):
1. **Proposal / Proposal Veto**: Functional line proposes; client-facing process team can veto
2. **Review / Audit**: Huawei University reviews capability; administrative superior audits fit
3. **Veto / Impeachment**: Ethics/Moral Compliance Committee holds veto on appointment and impeachment power during tenure

### Tier 2: Training-Battle Integration (训战提升能力)

- **Train as you fight**: All training forms, codes, and identifiers match real operational systems. No theoretical lectures; direct method instruction.
- **Exam = real results**: After training,学员 return to their units. Pass/fail is determined by whether their unit improves production/profit—not by classroom tests.
- **Zigzag rotation** (之字形成长): Mandatory cross-domain experience before promotion to general management. No "straight-line" single-function climbers.
  - Example path: R&D → GTS field → R&D → product line → region → strategy
- **Paid learning, self-driven**: Company provides standards and direction; employees own their development. Tuition reimbursement tied to passing certifications.

### Tier 3: Stable Civilian Core (稳定的职员队伍)

For the ~95% of deterministic work:
- **Civilian role definition**: Follow procedures precisely; responsible for command compliance, not outcome risk
- **Promotion path**: Based on workload size and error rate; no mandatory rotation
- **Job security**: No age ceiling; no forced mobility if competent and cost-effective
- **Psychological contract**: "Love your line, specialize in your line" (干一行、爱一行、专一行); deliver quality, efficiency, and low cost

Purpose: Free elites and soldiers from routine so they can focus on uncertainty; civilians provide the stable operational base.

### Flow Mechanisms (流动激发活力)

- **Horizontal rotation**: Business rotation (e.g., R&D → manufacturing → service) and position rotation for mid/senior cadres. No adjacent-domain experience → no promotion to general manager.
- **Vertical pressure**: Target responsibility + term limits. Failed targets → demotion/removal; no promotion within 1 year of demotion; no same-department deputy-to-chief promotions.
- **Reverse flow (Blue Army)**: Mandatory assignment to "opposition force" roles. No Red Army promotion without Blue Army experience. Cultivates open, two-sided strategic thinking.

## Output Format

Provide:
1. Selection scorecard with quality/performance/experience gates
2. Wolf-beaver role definitions and veto traits for a given team
3. Zigzag career path map for a target role
4. Civilian job architecture (levels, criteria, stability guarantees)
5. Rotation and exit flow rules for the unit

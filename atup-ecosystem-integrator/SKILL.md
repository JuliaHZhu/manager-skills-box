# atup-ecosystem-integrator

## Description

Extend the "acquire and share" (获取分享制) logic beyond the company's payroll to external partners, suppliers, distributors, developers, and ecosystem contributors. ATUP (Anyone TUP) turns equity-like incentives into a glue that binds external stakeholders to the company's platform — what Huawei calls "one cup of coffee absorbs cosmic energy, one bucket of paste sticks global wisdom."

Applicable when:
- The user wants to align suppliers, distributors, or ecosystem developers without giving them registered equity
- The company is building a platform, marketplace, or developer ecosystem
- The user asks about "获取分享制外延", "ATUP", "生态共赢", or "外部合伙人股权"
- The user wants to integrate external resources (渠道, 客户, 技术伙伴) into a shared value pool

Not applicable for pure internal employee incentives (use `tup-incentive-architect`) or for traditional supplier contracts with no co-creation element.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify ecosystem roles and value contributions

Map external stakeholders into one of four ATUP archetypes:

| Archetype | Example | Value Contribution | ATUP Linkage |
|-----------|---------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Channel Partner** | Distributor, reseller, agent | Sales volume, market penetration | Sales milestone × margin contribution |
| **Technology Partner** | ISV, SaaS developer, hardware modder | IP, integration, ecosystem apps | App revenue / API call volume / certification level |
| **Supply Partner** | Strategic supplier, co-manufacturer | Cost reduction, exclusivity, quality | Procurement savings × quality score |
| **Community Advocate** | KOL, power user, open-source contributor | Brand advocacy, content, bug hunting | Engagement index × influence reach |

### Step 2: Design the ATUP value pool

Unlike internal TUP (which draws from company profit), ATUP draws from **platform value creation**:

```
Platform Value Pool =
  (Revenue generated by ecosystem partners)
  − (Direct cost of serving those partners)
  × (Platform commission rate, e.g., 5-15%)
```

A portion of this pool (e.g., 30-50%) is allocated to ATUP; the remainder funds platform R&D and operations.

Example: Huawei's HarmonyOS developer fund commits 10B RMB; developers earn ATUP-like credits based on app downloads, user ratings, and ecosystem integration depth.

### Step 3: Build the ATUP accrual schedule

ATUP uses a shorter cycle than internal TUP (usually 2 years, not 5) because external partners have less organizational loyalty.

| Quarter | Accrual Rate | Event |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| Q1-Q2 | 25% of target units | Onboarding + first milestone |
| Q3-Q4 | 50% of target units | Sustained performance |
| Year 2 Q1-Q2 | 100% of target units | Full partnership depth |
| Year 2 Q3-Q4 | Terminal settlement + rollover decision | Cash payout or conversion to JV equity |

Settlement options at cycle end:
- **Cash payout**: Partner receives calculated value in cash; relationship continues on new cycle.
- **JV equity conversion**: Top-performing partners may convert ATUP value into minority equity in a regional JV or subsidiary.
- **Rollover**: Partner rolls value into next ATUP cycle with multiplier bonus (e.g., 1.2x).

### Step 4: Create the "shared black soil" platform contract

Huawei's metaphor: the platform is "black soil" (黑土地) where partners grow their own crops. The ATUP contract must define:

1. **Data transparency**: Partners see real-time dashboards of their contribution metrics.
2. **Fair arbitration**: Disputes over value attribution are resolved by a joint committee (company + partner representatives), not unilateral company decision.
3. **Non-exclusivity with preference**: Partners are not locked in, but ATUP multipliers reward exclusivity or deep integration.
4. **IP clarity**: Who owns what? Platform owns the infrastructure; partner owns their app/content; co-created IP is jointly licensed.

### Step 5: Launch the ATUP pilot

Recommended pilot structure:

| Phase | Duration | Scope | Success Metric |
|-------|----------|-------|---------------|
| **Pilot** | 6 months | 5-10 key partners | Partner satisfaction >70%; zero disputes |
| **Scale** | 12 months | 50 partners | Pool payout accuracy >95%; partner retention >80% |
| **Ecosystem** | 24 months | 200+ partners | Platform GMV growth >30% driven by ATUP partners |

Critical governance: Establish an **Ecosystem Value Committee** that includes external partner observers. This prevents the platform from unilaterally changing the ATUP algorithm.

## Example Prompts

### Prompt A: SaaS platform wants developer ecosystem
> "We run a B2B SaaS platform. We want independent developers to build plugins, but we don't want to give them equity in our parent company. How can ATUP work?"

Response outline:
1. Define plugin revenue attribution: when a customer subscribes because of a plugin, 20% of that revenue flows to the Plugin Value Pool.
2. Grant ATUP units to developers based on: plugin installs × active usage × customer rating.
3. 2-year cycle: Q1-Q2 25%, Q3-Q4 50%, Year 2 100% + terminal settlement.
4. Settlement: cash payout default; top 10% developers may convert to equity in a "Plugin Labs" subsidiary.
5. Contract: developer owns plugin IP; platform owns integration framework; revenue attribution audited quarterly.

### Prompt B: Manufacturer wants to bind distributors
> "We sell industrial equipment through regional distributors. They keep asking for exclusive territories and rebates. Can ATUP replace rebates with long-term alignment?"

Response outline:
1. Replace annual rebate with 2-year ATUP tied to: sales growth % + customer satisfaction score + spare-parts attach rate.
2. Distributors earn ATUP units; settlement can be cash or credit against future inventory purchases.
3. Exclusivity bonus: distributors who commit to exclusivity get 1.5x ATUP multiplier.
4. Terminal settlement: top distributor in each region may form a JV service company with manufacturer, converting ATUP to 20% JV equity.
5. Dashboard: give distributors real-time visibility into their ATUP accrual to sustain motivation.

### Prompt C: Association wants to align member companies
> "Our industry association has 80 member SMEs. We want them to share procurement and cross-sell, but everyone free-rides. Can ATUP create real collaboration?"

Response outline:
1. Create an association-level ATUP pool funded by 2% of cross-member transaction value.
2. Members earn ATUP for: referring deals, sharing procurement volume, contributing shared warehouse space.
3. ATUP translates to governance rights: members with highest ATUP balance get board seats in the association's operating entity.
4. Annual settlement: 50% cash, 50% rollover with 1.2x multiplier for members who stay active.
5. Critical: publish algorithm and audit results quarterly; trust is the scarcest resource in associations.

## Key Principles

1. **ATUP is external TUP**: Same logic (deferred, incremental, algorithmic), but applied to ecosystem contributors.
2. **Platform value, not company profit**: ATUP must be funded from the value partners co-create on the platform, not from the company's internal P&L.
3. **Shorter cycles for weaker bonds**: External partners get 2-year cycles, not 5, because their organizational loyalty is thinner.
4. **Transparency is the platform**: Partners must see their metrics in real time; opaque ATUP is worse than no ATUP.
5. **Conversion, not permanence**: Top partners convert ATUP to JV equity, not parent equity. Keep the parent clean.

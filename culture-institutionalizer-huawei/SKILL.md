# Huawei-Style Culture Institutionalizer

Build and sustain organizational culture through three Huawei-proven mechanisms: consensus through debate, habit through institutionalization, and atmosphere through leadership example. Converts abstract values into daily behavior and long-term customs.

## When to Use

- When rolling out or reinforcing core values across a growing organization
- When culture feels "stuck on the wall" rather than lived in behavior
- When new employee cohorts dilute existing cultural norms
- When you need a self-correcting mechanism (e.g., self-critique) to prevent organizational rigidity

## How It Works

### Pillar 1: Debate to Consensus (讨论达成共识)

Culture must be understood before it can be believed; believed before it can be practiced.

**Mechanics**:
- Treat culture as a **psychological contract**, not a poster.
- Use large-scale, open debate:
  - Publish draft values to all employees; request written feedback
  - Hold dedicated discussion sessions (e.g., Sunday study sessions)
  - Allow dissent and opposing views in formal forums ("Roman square" / 罗马广场)
  - Include families in early drafts (e.g., take the draft home, discuss, return with comments)
- Duration: Major cultural charters may take 1–3 years of iterative debate before finalization.
- Once finalized, declare: "The document's historical mission is complete because its ideas are now in our blood."

**Application**: Before launching a new values statement, run a 90-day internal debate cycle with anonymous feedback, leadership responses, and visible revisions.

### Pillar 2: Institutionalize into Habit (规范固化习惯)

Convert values into **processes and rules** that make the right behavior the easy behavior.

**Key systems**:
- **End-to-end process design**: Build "front line calls for firepower, rear provides support" workflows so that "customer-centric" is not a slogan but the only viable path.
- **Three-class employee framework**:
  - Ordinary workers: legal protections, fair pay, value > cost
  - General strivers: work hard, go home on time, balanced life
  - Effective strivers: the backbone; disproportionate rewards via bonuses and equity
- **Equity evolution** to sustain long-term struggle:
  - Virtual stock → saturated virtual stock (caps by level) → TUP (Time Unit Plan, 5-year deferred+escalating grants that reset to zero after year 5)
  - Purpose: prevent a rentier class from forming; ensure continuous contribution is required for continuous gain

**Governance rule**: "Promote self-discipline, perfect external discipline" (促进自律，完善他律). Start with trust; reinforce with transparent systems.

### Pillar 3: Atmosphere Through Example (营造文化氛围)

Culture is contagion. Shape it through visible, consistent leadership behavior.

**Leadership behaviors**:
- **Top leader example**: Visit hardship posts personally; publicly admit errors (e.g., publish internal critical letters about oneself); maintain daily learning habits
- **Veteran reinforcement**: Celebrate heroes loudly and visibly (medals, ceremonies, banners). Keep advanced-role models at 60–70% of the population so the majority feels recognized, while the bottom 30–40% faces natural attrition.
- **New employee induction**: "When hemp grows among straight stalks, it grows straight without support" (蓬生麻中，不扶自直). Ensure new hires are immersed in an environment where the prevailing behavior is the desired behavior.

**Anti-pattern**: Do not rely on slogans, slide decks, or annual town halls alone. Culture is transmitted through observed behavior, especially under stress.

## Output Format

Produce a culture institutionalization roadmap containing:
1. Debate calendar and feedback mechanism design
2. Process/structural changes that hardwire each core value into workflow
3. Three-class talent segmentation and corresponding incentive alignment
4. Leadership example checklist (specific behaviors, frequency, visibility)
5. New-hire immersion plan and 90-day cultural assimilation metrics

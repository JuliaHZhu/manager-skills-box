# HR Blueprint Strategist

Apply the Huawei HR Management Outline 2.0 (人力资源管理纲要2.0) framework for top-down human resource architecture. Focuses on keeping the organization vibrant, balancing material and spiritual drives, and designing distributed governance under shared values.

## When to Use

- When redesigning HR strategy for a maturing or multi-business organization
- When the company is shifting from a single-business "big tree" to a multi-business "forest"
- When you need to align HR policy with strategic direction (SP/BP) rather than administrative convenience
- When simplifying HR processes while maintaining control over risk and compliance

## How It Works

### Core Objective: Organization Full of Vitality (让组织充满活力)

The ultimate deliverable of HR is not compliance or cost control; it is **organizational vitality**. Achieve this through three capabilities:

1. **Aspirational energy**: Mission, vision, and shared values that elevate motives beyond compensation
2. **Perception and responsiveness**: Insight into change, rapid decision-making, and a culture that tolerates trial-and-error
3. **Creation and integration**: Individual and team creativity, strategic decisiveness, resource integration, execution speed

### Dual Drive: Material + Spiritual (物质文明与精神文明)

- **Material**: Optimize "get-and-share" (获取分享制). Short-term incentives target grain production; long-term incentives target sustained contribution. Labor returns should exceed capital returns (target ratio ~3:1).
- **Spiritual**: Vision-driven motivation, not money-only. Use honor systems, medals, and ceremonies as productive forces. Allow points-based honor systems that tie to long-term benefits (e.g., higher retention of equity post-retirement).

### Three Objects of HR Management (三个对象)

Break the traditional "select-train-use-retain" modules. Manage three objects:

1. **Cadres (干部)**: Mission + responsibility + strategic insight + campaign control + sacrifice spirit. Senior cadres must "look up at the stars" (strategic thinking) and have frontline combat experience. Promote from successful practice only.
2. **Talent (人才)**: Top talent acquisition, ecosystem openness (not own-but-use), differentiated management by business and role. Emphasize potential over experience in fast-changing environments.
3. **Organization (组织)**: Business determines organization. Shift from "one big tree" to "a forest" under shared values (sky) and shared platform (ground), with differentiated business operations in between.

### Operating Model: Platform + Business Team (平台+业务团队)

- **Front line**: Small, empowered combat teams ("squad leader's war" / 班长战争). Contract adjudication close to the customer.
- **Platform**: Central platform simplifies strategic oversight, rule-setting, and supervision; front platform supports execution.
- **Governance**: Three central authorities remain vertically controlled (funds, accounting, audit). All other powers are delegated to the front line within compliance boundaries.
- **Trust-based management**: Reduce process constraints and KPI micromanagement for the majority of employees who demonstrate responsibility. Simplify reporting; judge by results within guardrails.

### Key Transitions from Outline 1.0 to 2.0

| Element | 1.0 Era | 2.0 Era |
|---|---|---|
| Incentive logic | Evaluate-then-distribute | Get-and-share |
| Management basis | Control and audit | Trust and simplify |
| Organization | Single tree | Forest (distributed) |
| Talent view | Human resources | Talent + super-talent |
| Drive | Primarily material | Material + spiritual dual drive |
| Cadre growth | Experience-heavy | Potential + leadership emphasized |

## Output Format

Produce a strategic HR blueprint containing:
1. Vitality diagnosis (current state vs. desired state across the 3 capabilities)
2. Dual-drive incentive architecture with labor-vs-capital ratio targets
3. Three-object management plan (cadre pipeline, talent ecosystem, org design)
4. Platform + frontline governance map with retained vs. delegated powers
5. 12-month simplification roadmap (processes to cut, trust boundaries to define)

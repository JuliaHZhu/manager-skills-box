## When to Activate

Use when the user is considering a major business transformation — brand repositioning, moving upmarket, dropping low-margin legacy business, building core technology in-house, or entering new channels. Also triggers when user discusses "from ODM to OEM", "brand upgrade", "放弃低端", "自研", or "从零起飞".

**Key triggers**: 业务转型 / business transformation / 战略转型 / 品牌升级 / 自有品牌 / 中高端转型 / 战略取舍 / 从零起飞 / 针尖战略 / 主航道 / 砍掉低端 / 自研投入 / 转型阵痛

**Not for**: minor product-line adjustments, cost-cutting without strategic repositioning, or pure marketing campaigns without business-model change.

## Core Methodology

Huawei Terminal's rise from "free phone bundled with carrier plan" to global top-2 smartphone brand followed seven strategic decisions, each embodying a transformation principle.

### The Seven Decisions & Their Principles

1. **Positioning determines status (定位决定地位)** — ODM white-label → OEM self-brand
   - Without independent branding, you remain invisible to consumers and can never control experience.
   - Short-term pain: lost carrier orders, revenue miss, zero-bonus year (the "zero-takeoff award").
   - Long-term gain: brand autonomy, pricing power, direct consumer relationship.

2. **Vision determines altitude (眼界决定境界)** — Low-end → Mid-to-high-end
   - Mediocrity in the low-end is a trap; only premium pricing funds innovation.
   - Initial failures (Ascend P1, D series) are tuition fees. Persevere through iterative learning.
   - Key enabler: CEO personally selling in retail stores to understand consumers firsthand.

3. **Strategy is about trade-offs (战略就是取舍)** — Abandon ultra-low-end feature phones
   - Internal enemies are created when you cut profitable legacy business.
   - Feature phones consumed nearly 100 R&D teams, fragmented by network standard. Cutting them consolidated platform power.
   - Resource liberation > incremental revenue from legacy.

4. **The hardest road is the shortcut (最难的路才是捷径)** — Insist on self-developed chips
   - Buying chips is cheap; designing chips is a "bottomless pit" of money and time.
   - K3V2 was mocked for overheating, but the lesson was: "It is our own chip; this road we must walk to the end."
   - After 10 years, Kirin chips shipped nearly 400 million units. Only Huawei, Apple, and Samsung design full SoC for phones.

5. **Self-improvement invites divine aid (自强者天助之)** — E-commerce channel
   - Huawei brand phones almost got renamed because early flagship e-commerce models failed.
   - Honor sub-brand succeeded where Huawei brand initially struggled, proving the power of focused channel strategy.
   - Breakthrough came when Mate 7 went viral; e-commerce then became a core pillar.

6. **Small steps accumulate to a thousand miles (积跬步致千里)** — EMUI deep optimization
   - Instead of skin-deep UI changes, Huawei rewrote Android底层 code to solve卡顿 (lag after prolonged use).
   - Over $200M invested in EMUI software; later open-sourced F2FS file system to Google.
   - Result: simulated 18-month heavy use led to only 5% performance degradation.

7. **No one is born great (没有生来伟大)** — Hardware世界第一
   - From 7nm Kirin 980 to Leica triple-camera, 40W supercharge, graphene cooling.
   - Built global labs (Japan for materials, Russia for thermal, Paris for aesthetics) to attract top talent.

### Transformation Operating Principles

| Principle | Meaning | Application |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Strategic patience** | 10-year horizon for chip R&D; do not expect quick wins on foundational bets. | Set multi-year milestones, shield from short-term profit pressure. |
| **Pain acceptance** | First year of brand switch: missed target by $200M+, executives forfeited bonuses. | Create ritualized acceptance of short-term sacrifice (zero-takeoff award). |
| **CEO immersion** |余承东 stood in retail stores as a "sales uncle" to understand consumers. | Leaders must touch the frontline during transformation, not delegate entirely. |
| **Platform consolidation** | After cutting feature phones, 100 teams merged into unified hardware/software platforms. | Every cut must be followed by consolidation, not just elimination. |
| **Open contribution** | F2FS donated back to Android ecosystem. | Build industry reputation even while competing. |

### STOP Checkpoint

Before approving a transformation plan, verify:
- [ ] Is the transformation anchored in a "main channel" (主航道) with large enough TAM?
- [ ] Have we identified what legacy business to kill, and who will resist?
- [ ] Is there a "zero-takeoff" ritual to signal seriousness and share pain?
- [ ] Does the CEO personally spend time at the new frontline?
- [ ] Are we investing in hard capabilities (chips, software, talent) or just rebranding?
- [ ] Is the timeline measured in years, not quarters?

## Output Format

When activated, provide:
1. A **transformation readiness scan** against the seven principles.
2. A **legacy-business kill list** with stakeholder impact map.
3. A **capability investment roadmap** (what to build in-house vs. buy).
4. A **pain-sharing mechanism** design.
5. A **3-year milestone tracker** with leading and lagging indicators.

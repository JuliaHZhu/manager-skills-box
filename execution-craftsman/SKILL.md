## Core Methodology

Huawei's work methodology bridges individual discipline and organizational execution through five interconnected systems.

### System 1: SMART Goal Discipline

Before any work begins, five questions must be answered:
1. **What** — exactly what outcome?
2. **How** — what is the method?
3. **How much** — what is the quantitative target?
4. **Where** — in what scope/context?
5. **Why** — what is the strategic rationale?

Never "start shooting before aiming."

**SMART criteria applied Huawei-style:**
- **Specific**: not "improve sales" but "increase accessory attach rate in retail stores to 20%."
- **Measurable**: three quantified indicators — time volume (时量), quantity (数量), quality (质量).
- **Actionable**: goals must be reachable by the person who owns them.
- **Results-oriented**: every goal ties to a business result, not an activity.
- **Time-bound**: deadlines are non-negotiable.

**"Jump to pick fruit, not stars" (跳起来摘果子，而不是摘星星)**
- Goals should stretch but not break.
- Investigate feasibility before committing; Ren Zhengfei himself plans only two years ahead.

### System 2: Upper Level Makes Momentum, Lower Level Makes Reality (上层作势，基层做实)

- **Upper level (作势)**: defines strategy, sets direction, creates conditions.
- **Lower level (做实)**: executes with precision, delivers tangible results.
- A plan without execution is decoration; execution without plan is waste.

### System 3: Responsibility to the Task, Not the Boss (对事负责制)

- **对人负责制** (responsibility to person): convergent system; everything escalates to the boss.
- **对事负责制** (responsibility to task): expansion system; the person who understands the problem has the authority to solve it.
- Rules: if a rule or precedent exists, do not ask for permission. Act and let the process record it.
- Exception: for major利益 events, retain upward notification.

### System 4: The Four Standardizations (四化管理)

| Standardization | Meaning | Execution Rule |
|-----------------|---------|----------------|
| **职业化** (Professionalization) | Same task, same conditions, lower cost. | Efficiency is the measure. |
| **规范化** (Standardization) | Follow rules and processes strictly. | Build a stable management system. |
| **表格化** (Tabulation) | Use tables and charts for all data. | Decisions must be data-driven. |
| **模板化** (Templatization) | Create templates for recurring work. | Reduce reinvention; increase speed. |

### System 5: Focus and Pressure (压强原则)

- **Focus**: concentrate resources on a limited work面 until mastery.
- **Pressure principle**: apply concentrated force through a single aperture (力出一孔).
- Do not disperse energy across too many small projects.
- "You want to be expert at everything = expert at nothing."

### Supporting Habits

1. **Write down the original dream** — remind yourself why you joined; maintain desire for the goal.
2. **Solve easy problems first** — build momentum; small wins lead to big wins.
3. **Do important things first** — leaders grasp main contradictions.
4. **Batch trivial tasks** — handle琐碎 centrally to protect deep-work blocks.
5. **Follow process** — process exists to prevent reinvention of wheels.
6. **Simplify** — per Tracy's Law: difficulty ∝ (steps)². Reduce steps.
7. **Use correct methods to do right things** — efficiency (正确地做事) vs. effectiveness (做正确的事).
8. **Rest no more than 5 minutes** — breaks restore energy but must not break flow state.

## STOP Checkpoint

Before accepting a work plan, verify:
- [ ] Are the five pre-work questions answered?
- [ ] Are targets quantified by time, quantity, and quality?
- [ ] Is the goal a "reachable fruit" not an unreachable star?
- [ ] Is execution ownership clear (对事负责制)?
- [ ] Have templates been created or reused for recurring work?
- [ ] Is energy concentrated, not dispersed?

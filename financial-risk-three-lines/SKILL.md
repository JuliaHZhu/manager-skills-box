## Purpose
Design a financial risk management system that protects the organization from systemic threats without suffocating business agility. Based on Huawei's "4×3" risk framework (three risk categories, three defense lines, three-layer review, triangular linkage), this skill balances control with empowerment.

## When to Use
- When financial crises (liquidity, fraud, currency, credit) have caused or could cause major damage
- When rapid growth has outpaced risk management capabilities
- When audit findings reveal recurring control weaknesses
- When expanding into new markets with unfamiliar regulatory and currency risks
- When designing a CFO organization's risk oversight mandate
- When the business complains that finance "blocks everything" rather than enabling safe growth

## When NOT to Use
- For pure insurance or hedging instrument selection (too narrow)
- For crisis response after the fact (use crisis-deterrence-survivor)
- When the organization has no operating history to analyze
- For non-financial operational risks (e.g., factory safety) without financial impact

## Core Framework: The 4×3 Risk Architecture

### Dimension 1 — Three Risk Categories

| Category | Definition | Examples |
|----------|------------|----------|
| **Strategic Risk** | Risks arising from major strategic choices | Market entry failure, M&A integration, technology obsolescence |
| **Operational Risk** | Risks in day-to-day business execution | Contract execution errors, supply chain disruption, customer defaults |
| **Financial Risk** | Risks directly impacting financial position | Liquidity crunch, currency volatility, interest rate exposure, credit risk |

**Management Cycle**: For each risk, execute **Identify → Assess → Respond → Monitor → Report**.

### Dimension 2 — Three Lines of Defense

**Line 1: Business Managers (流程责任)**
- Role: Own the risk in their operations. Every business manager is the first control point.
- Mechanism: Build controls INTO business processes, not ON TOP of them.
- Huawei practice: "流程内控" — control requirements embedded in workflow design.

**Line 2: Internal Control & Oversight (组织责任)**
- Role: Monitor and support Line 1 in fulfilling their control duties.
- Mechanism: Risk reports, control testing, policy guidance.
- Huawei practice: Finance监控 + 道德遵从委员会 create a "field" of compliance atmosphere.

**Line 3: Internal Audit (威慑责任)**
- Role: Independent, random, point-in-time verification.
- Mechanism: Unpredictable audits of transactions, contracts, and accounts.
- Huawei practice: Audit is the "司法部队" — judicial force. It does not need to be comprehensive; it needs to be feared.

**Key Insight**: Do NOT overload Line 3. If every control gap is pushed to audit, the audit organization would become unaffordably large. Audit's job is deterrence, not daily policing.

### Dimension 3 — Three-Layer Review (三层审结)

**Layer 1: Independent CFO Organization**
- Every manager has a dedicated CFO supporting them.
- CFO ensures that business decisions are evaluated through a financial and risk lens.

**Layer 2: Centralized Accounting & Treasury**
- Accounting team verifies that all booked transactions reflect business reality (账实相符).
- Treasury team reconciles all bank accounts daily against internal records (账账相符).

**Layer 3: Audit Confirmation**
- Random deep-dives into specific transactions or contracts.
- Creates pervasive fear of detection, which is cheaper than comprehensive inspection.

### Dimension 4 — Triangular Linkage (三角联动)

| Function | Scope | Method |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Audit (审计) | Point | Spot checks, case-by-case |
| Finance Monitoring (财务监控) | Line | End-to-end process oversight, continuous |
| Ethics & Compliance (道德遵从) | Plane/Field | Cultural environment, self-discipline |

**Combined Effect**: The organization is surrounded by control — specific points (audit), continuous lines (finance), and ambient cultural pressure (ethics). This makes comprehensive cheating statistically unlikely and culturally unacceptable.

## Control Philosophy: Enablement, Not Obstruction

### Principle 1: Control Serves Authorization
"The purpose of control is not control itself, but the institutional guarantee of reasonable authorization and effective execution."

- **面 (Plane) control**: Ensures sustainable and stable operations.
- **线 (Line) control**: Keeps key and systemic risks at extremely low levels.
- **点 (Point) control**: Helps managers mature through self-reflection and self-improvement.

### Principle 2: Frontline Command Rights + Backline Oversight Rights
- Those close to the battlefield have field command authority — they must dare to exercise power.
- Those with global management responsibility have oversight authority — they authorize reasonably and control effectively.

### Principle 3: Differentiated Service by Credit Grade
Finance services should be differentiated based on the business unit's historical control performance:

| Credit Grade | Service Level |
|--------------|---------------|
| High integrity | Fast-track, post-audit, minimal friction |
| Medium integrity | Standard process, periodic review |
| Low integrity | Pre-audit, enhanced documentation, slower processing |

This creates positive incentives: better control behavior → faster service → more business agility.

## Risk Management Tactics

### Tactic 1: Financial Blue Team (财务蓝军)
Establish independent risk-control centers in financial hubs (e.g., London, Tokyo, New York).
- Role: Challenge "Red Team" (business/ finance leadership) assumptions.
- Methods: Stress-test financial architecture, strategy, and operating rhythm.
- Goal: Prevent systemic risk from building up undetected.

### Tactic 2: Pre-Event / In-Event / Post-Event Control
Embed control at three moments:
- **Before**: Authorization gates, budget checks, compliance validation.
- **During**: Real-time monitoring dashboards, exception alerts.
- **After**: Reconciliation, audit sampling, variance analysis.

### Tactic 3: Risk Report as Public Billboard
Finance publishes risk reports prominently (intranet, management meetings).
- Transparency puts pressure on responsible organizations to act.
- Finance's role is revelation, NOT execution of fixes.
- Business owns the fix; finance checks the fix result.

## STOP Checkpoints
Before deploying a financial risk framework, verify:
1. [ ] Are controls embedded IN business processes (Line 1), not bolted ON TOP by audit (Line 3)?
2. [ ] Does every business manager have a CFO partner who reviews decisions before commitment?
3. [ ] Is audit scoped for deterrence (random, feared) rather than comprehensiveness (expensive, slow)?
4. [ ] Do we differentiate financial service speed based on the unit's control track record?
5. [ ] Is there a "Blue Team" function that can challenge CFO and business leadership assumptions?
6. [ ] Are risk reports public and transparent, with clear ownership for remediation?

## Output Format
When invoked, provide:
1. **Risk Landscape**: Categorize current exposures into Strategic / Operational / Financial.
2. **Defense Line Diagnosis**: Which line is overloaded or underdeveloped?
3. **Control Design**: Embedded process controls for the top 3 risks.
4. **Three-Layer Review Map**: CFO → Accounting/Treasury → Audit roles and handoffs.
5. **Credit-Based Service Matrix**: Differentiated finance service levels by control grade.
6. **Implementation Roadmap**: 90-day quick wins + 12-month structural changes.

# Consulting Value Maximizer

Maximize return on management consulting engagements using Huawei's methodology: trust-first, price-no-barrier, full-time internal teams, milestone rigor, and systematic knowledge transfer. Use when hiring consultants or managing transformation projects.

## When to Use

Activate when the user discusses:
- 咨询公司 / management consulting / 顾问
- 咨询项目怎么管 / consulting project management
- 咨询落地 / consulting implementation
- 咨询选型 / 怎么选咨询公司 / selecting consulting partners
- 咨询价值 / 为什么咨询没效果 / why consulting fails
- 里程碑管理 / milestone management
- PMO / 项目管理办公室
- 知识内化 / 要鱼更要渔 / knowledge transfer

Do NOT activate for: pure IT software implementation (unless management change involved), temporary staffing, or internal projects without external consultants.

## Core Concepts

### 1. Trust Architecture

**Trust-first (信任在前)**:
- 任正非: "要么不请，请进来不信任，还不如不请。拜神不信，一定不灵"
- Either don't hire, or hire and trust fully
- CEO completes trust assessment BEFORE signing; not delegated to middle management
- **Trust-from-top (信任由上)**: Consulting is a "boss project" (老板工程)
- Brain decides medicine; taste buds may resist — can't let middle management veto

**Contrast with typical companies**:
- Typical: Hire → Challenge → Play games → Consultant becomes defensive
- Huawei: Trust → Enable → Consultant becomes proactive

### 2. Environment Design

**Make consultants at home**:
- Best office locations, IBM headquarters style renovation
- Car service, lasting respect, no怠慢
- Consultant is CEO's proxy — full authority on project domain
- IBM consultants罢免华为不配合的高级干部十数人
- Consultant's pride, belonging, security > Huawei employees

**Why**: Happy百灵鸟 sings beautifully; unhappy bird cannot be forced to produce精华思想.

### 3. Price Philosophy

**任正非 on IBM's $48M quote**:
- CFO: "Let's negotiate down."
- 任正非: "You negotiate price; can you bear project risk?"
- Final question to IBM: "Do you have confidence to do this well?"
- IBM: "Yes." → Signed at quoted price.

**Total investment**: Nearly 2 billion RMB (contract + implementation + IT tools)

**Result during crisis**: When Huawei R&D高层集体出走, 300 senior IBM consultants stepped in. Project didn't中断; system carried operations.

**10-year result (2008)**: 20x growth; R&D cycle shortened 50%; R&D cost reduced 30%.

**Key insight**: If Huawei had negotiated down, IBM might not have deployed 300 senior managers during crisis. Budget determines time quality; time quality determines outcome.

### 4. Correct Consulting View

**Consultant is NOT**:
- Supplier or service provider
- Magician who solves everything
- Replacement for executive thinking

**Consultant IS**:
- Partner
- Tool and methodology provider
- Experience and knowledge sharer
- Innovation inspiration point
- Internal change catalyst

**任正非**: "咨询公司领进门，修行提升靠企业" — Consultant opens door; cultivation is up to the enterprise.

### 5. Project Management Office (PMO)

**Why PMO**: Someone must coordinate multiple projects, allocate resources, ensure alignment with strategy.

**PMO functions**:
- Total project coordination
- Resource integration
- Project portfolio management
- Path map development
- Risk control

### 6. Project Path Map (项目路径图)

**Development process**:
1. Review strategic planning with consultant — identify gaps
2. Agree on core problems to solve
3. Evaluate necessity, urgency, feasibility of each project
4. Consider integration between projects
5. Prioritize and balance portfolio
6. Establish rolling update mechanism

**Principle**: Limited resources to important, bounded, non-repetitive, pioneering, difficult matters.

### 7. Consultant Selection

**Three core criteria**:
1. **Hiring purpose**: Specific goals, success measures, capability match
2. **Relevant experience**: Firm type, industry position, core strengths, global experience, team成功案例
3. **Team composition**: Background, capability, experience, project focus

**Guard against three拍**:
- 拍胸脯 (promise without thought)
- 拍脑袋 (decide rashly)
- 拍屁股 (leave without finishing)

**Best practice**: Long-term relationship (3-5+ years) with 1-2 primary firms; top-tier specialists in specific domains.

### 8. Consultant Integration (融化咨询师)

**Three phases**:
1. **Understand enterprise**: Company overview, culture, business process training; frontline调研; management interviews
2. **Respect and care**: Treat as teachers; CEO participates in detailed PPT reviews; meals and personal connection
3. **Interactive fusion**: 
   - Pairing (结对子): Daily co-working
   - Activities: Weekly after-work文体 activities
   - Training/sharing: Both directions — consultant teaches methodology; employees share domain knowledge

### 9. Knowledge Transfer (要鱼，更要渔)

**Huawei tactics**:
- **Full-time internal team**: Employees fully seconded to project; reporting,考核 within project
- **Surround tactic**: Employees highly cooperative; respect generates consultant willingness to share
- **Self-produced PPTs**: Employees write and present; consultant guides thinking. Pressure forces rapid learning.

**Principle**: Don't become dependent. Don't邯郸学步. Internalize knowledge.

### 10. Milestone and Risk Management

**Milestone management**:
- Define milestones at kickoff
- Stage meetings and reports = surgical切口 into project横截面
- Project committee (高管 + experts) reviews
- **Rule**: One milestone failure = stop next phase until resolved

**Risk management**:
- Identify events affecting process, efficiency, targets
- Complete risk mechanism minimizes loss

### 11. Implementation Guarantee

**Three key elements for落地 success**:
1. **Frequent communication**: Unify thinking,坚定信念
2. **Encourage teams**: Let subordinates放手一搏; monitor progress
3. **CEO leads by example**: Spend time and energy on new business

**Post-project tracking**:
- Assign承接部门 before project closes
- Use KPI to guarantee落地
- Project office tracks异常; requires汇报
- Three controls: medium/long-term tracking (point), landing summary (面), landing rating (考核)

## Operational Workflow

When user is engaging or planning to engage consultants:

### Step 1: Self-Clarification
- Can YOU clearly articulate what you want? (Entrepreneur must think first)
- Establish PMO if multiple projects

### Step 2: Path Map Development
- With consultant, develop multi-year project roadmap
- Prioritize by strategy alignment, urgency, feasibility
- Define boundaries and integration points

### Step 3: Partner Selection
- Long-term view; seek best team, not cheapest price
- Evaluate team composition, not just firm brand
- Check for three拍 red flags

### Step 4: Trust Setup
- CEO personally completes trust assessment
- Create superior working environment
- Grant consultant real authority (can remove obstructing executives)

### Step 5: Integration & Transfer
- Full-time internal team pairing
- Employees write PPTs and present
- Two-way knowledge sharing

### Step 6: Milestone Rigor
- One-strike rule: milestone fail = next phase halt
- Quantified evaluation: cost, schedule, changes, pass rate, satisfaction

### Step 7: Landing & Legacy
- Assign承接 department with KPI before close
- Annual复盘 with both sides
- Document lessons; prevent重复错误

## Key Distinctions

**Trust vs Blind Faith**:
- Trust: Give authority and environment; hold to milestones
- Blind faith: No oversight, no milestones

**Price vs Value**:
- Price focus: Negotiate down; get compliance-level effort
- Value focus: Pay for senior time; get transformation-level effort

**Dependency vs Internalization**:
- Dependency: Consultant does everything; leaves; nothing remains
- Internalization: Consultant teaches; employees own; capability remains

# striver-ownership-culture

## Description

Build an ownership culture where "strivers" (奋斗者) are identified, celebrated, and economically enriched — while "passengers" (坐车人) naturally see their relative share decline. Based on Huawei's cultural infrastructure: "Don't let Lei Feng suffer losses; give Lei Feng something to lose; make everyone want to be Lei Feng."

Applicable when:
- The company has generous equity or profit-sharing but declining work ethic
- The user wants to define "striver" beyond vague slogans
- The user needs to link cultural values to concrete economic differentiation
- The user asks about "奋斗者", "不让雷锋吃亏", "利益共同体", or "命运共同体"

Not applicable when the organization lacks basic performance measurement (culture cannot substitute for metrics) or when the founder's own work ethic is visibly declining.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define the striver standard

A "striver" is not someone who works long hours. Huawei's explicit criteria:

| Dimension | Striver Indicator | Passenger Indicator |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Willingness** | Volunteers for hardship posts, new ventures, or crisis assignments | Declines mobility; avoids unfamiliar tasks |
| **Contribution** | Sustained A/B+ performance over 2+ years | Fluctuating or C-rated performance |
| **Values** | Aligns with company core values in behavior reviews | Values-compliant on paper only; political operator |
| **Sacrifice** | Accepts short-term personal loss for long-term collective gain | Optimizes personal convenience over team outcome |

Operationalization: The "striver" designation is not a one-time award. It is reviewed annually by the Administration Team (AT) or equivalent committee. Employees self-declare as strivers; managers verify; exceptions are escalated.

### Step 2: Build the three-tier economic community

Use economic instruments to create graduated community levels:

```
Tier 1: Interest Community (利益共同体)
   └─ Instrument: Salary + bonus (immediate, transactional)
   └─ Membership: All employees who meet basic performance
   └─ Message: "Work hard, get paid fairly."

Tier 2: TUP Community (中期共同体)
   └─ Instrument: TUP units (3-5 year deferred upside)
   └─ Membership: Strivers who qualify by performance + values
   └─ Message: "Stay and build; the future belongs to builders."

Tier 3: Destiny Community (命运共同体)
   └─ Instrument: ESOP / registered equity (long-term ownership)
   └─ Membership: Top-tier strivers with 5+ years of sustained contribution
   └─ Message: "You are not just an employee; you are a co-owner."
```

Progression rule: Movement between tiers is bidirectional. A Tier 3 member who drops to C performance for 2 years loses eligibility for new ESOP grants and sees TUP accrual suspended. A Tier 1 member who rises to sustained A performance enters Tier 2.

### Step 3: Differentiate economic rights by tier

| Right | Tier 1 (General) | Tier 2 (Striver - TUP) | Tier 3 (Destiny - ESOP) |
|-------|-----------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| Salary | Market-competitive | Market-competitive + 10-20% premium | Market-competitive + long-term wealth |
| Bonus | Standard formula | Enhanced pool access | Dividend + appreciation |
| Equity-like | None | TUP units (5-year cycle) | ESOP shares (virtual or registered) |
| Mobility | Voluntary | Expected for key posts | Mandatory for senior roles |
| Protection | Labor law only | TUP clawback protections | Share repurchase at NAV |

### Step 4: Run the "striver certification" ritual

Huawei makes striver status explicit and public:

1. **Self-declaration**: Employee submits a "Striver Application" stating why they qualify.
2. **Manager endorsement**: Direct manager adds evidence-based confirmation.
3. **Peer review**: Colleagues may raise objections (anonymously) if the applicant's behavior contradicts striver criteria.
4. **AT ratification**: The Administration Team votes; simple majority required.
5. **Public listing**: Striver names are published internally; this creates both honor and accountability.

Frequency: Annual, aligned with the performance review cycle.

### Step 5: Anchor culture in stories, not slogans

Huawei's cultural transmission relies on **stories**, not posters:

- **The Pakistan bomb story**: A trainer continued teaching after a suicide blast nearby. Symbol: culture is behavior under fire.
- **The ballet shoe**: Beautiful feet that are actually scarred. Symbol: excellence requires sacrifice.
- **The thousand-hand Guanyin**: Seamless teamwork. Symbol: no single hero, only synchronized teams.

Action: Collect 3-5 stories from the company's own history that illustrate striver behavior. Retell them in onboarding, town halls, and leader meetings. Replace generic values posters with story-based case studies.

### Step 6: Measure culture health quarterly

| Indicator | Target | Red Flag |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Striver application rate | >60% of eligible employees | <40% (culture perceived as unfair) |
| Striver-to-passenger income ratio | 2:1 or higher | <1.5:1 (insufficient differentiation) |
| Voluntary attrition among strivers | <5% annually | >10% (economic rights not compelling) |
| Values-violation incidents | Declining trend | Rising trend (hypocrisy detected) |

If red flags appear, the problem is usually not communication but **measurement fairness**. Employees become cynical when they see non-strivers rewarded.

## Example Prompts

### Prompt A: New CEO wants to revive work ethic
> "Our company became comfortable after a good decade. People leave at 5pm. How do we rebuild a striving culture without becoming toxic?"

Response outline:
1. Diagnose: likely Tier 2/3 economic rights have become automatic; striver certification has atrophied.
2. Re-launch explicit striver application process with AT ratification.
3. Shift new economic value to TUP (Tier 2) and make ESOP (Tier 3) contingent on re-certification every 2 years.
4. Increase striver-to-passenger income ratio to 2:1+ through TUP differentiation.
5. Launch "story collection": find 3 internal stories of sacrifice/teamwork/excellence and amplify them monthly.
6. Guardrail: monitor hours-worked vs. outcomes; striving is about contribution, not presenteeism.

### Prompt B: Family business with lazy relatives
> "My relatives are shareholders but don't work. Other employees resent them. How do we apply 'striver' logic here?"

Response outline:
1. Hard truth: family shareholders with registered equity cannot be "fired" from ownership easily.
2. Separate economic streams: create a "working striver dividend" funded from operating profit, distributed only to active employees; existing dividends continue but are relabeled "historical return."
3. For new family entrants: mandatory 2-year TUP trial before any equity eligibility; no automatic inheritance of striver status.
4. Cultural move: founder publicly states that "relative status is neutral; only contribution status matters for new value."
5. Legal check: review shareholder agreements for any clauses that discriminate against non-family employees; amend if necessary.

### Prompt C: Multinational with local staff who feel excluded
> "Our overseas local staff feel the ESOP is only for Chinese expats. How do we make them feel like owners too?"

Response outline:
1. Diagnosis: ESOP purchase requirements may exclude locals who cannot afford shares or access loans.
2. Solution: Launch TUP for all local staff first (no purchase required); this is the "Tier 2" entry point.
3. Localize the striver criteria: hardship post = local context (e.g., working in remote regions, learning HQ language, cross-cultural project leadership).
4. Make TUP settlement available in local currency with hedging support if exchange rates are volatile.
5. Publish local striver stories: highlight non-Chinese employees who exemplify company values.

## Key Principles

1. **Striver is a verb, not a noun**: It is certified annually based on behavior and results, not granted permanently based on history.
2. **Economic differentiation is the proof**: If strivers and passengers earn the same, the culture is a slogan.
3. **Three-tier community creates aspiration**: Everyone starts at Tier 1; the path to Tier 3 is visible and meritocratic.
4. **Stories beat posters**: Culture lives in narratives of real behavior under pressure.
5. **Founder must be the first striver**: If the founder's own effort declines, no certification process can compensate.

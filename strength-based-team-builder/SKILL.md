## Core Methodology

Huawei explicitly recruits "歪瓜裂枣" — people with prominent strengths and equally prominent weaknesses. The organizational design compensates for individual weaknesses through team composition, not through individual remediation.

### The "Lopsided Talent" Philosophy (歪瓜裂枣)

- Most companies screen out candidates with visible flaws; Huawei screens **in** those with outsized strengths even if they have clear deficits.
- Training should make a person's **strength longer**, not patch their **weaknesses**.
- Weaknesses are compensated by **team**, not by individual development.

### The Wolf-and-Bie Organization (狼狈组织)

The name comes from a fable: wolf has aggression; bie (a mythical supporting creature) has cunning and stability. Neither is complete alone; paired, they are formidable.

| Role | Trait | Contribution |
|------|-------|------------|
| **Wolf (狼)** | Aggressive, opportunity-seeking, external-facing | Hunts opportunities, drives revenue, expands territory |
| **Bie (狈)** | Meticulous, supportive, internal-facing | Builds systems, manages operations, ensures stability |

**Application rules:**
1. For every team leader strong in external opportunity capture, pair a deputy strong in internal management.
2. When a frontline order arrives, the Bie (back office) rapidly prepares proposals and mobilizes resources; the Wolf closes the deal.
3. Do not expect one person to be both Wolf and Bie. That expectation is the root of burnout and mediocrity.

### Three Characteristics of Wolfish Teams

Huawei's "wolf nature" is not about aggression alone. It has three precise features:

1. **Continuous value creation (创造生存基础)**
   - The team must constantly generate the economic basis for the firm's survival.
   - No output = no purpose. Teams that cannot deliver grain are disbanded.

2. **Results orientation (结果导向)**
   - In the face of competition and difficulty, the team must deliver regardless of whether all conditions are perfect.
   - If the company had no gaps, the team would have no unique value.

3. **Team cooperation (团队合作)**
   - Individuals are small; the team can achieve what no individual can.
   - Collective execution overrides individual brilliance.

### Practical Team-Building Protocol

1. **Map the task's required strengths** — identify the 3-5 critical capabilities needed for success.
2. **Inventory existing members' peaks** — list each person's top 2 strengths, not their weaknesses.
3. **Design compensatory pairs** — for each strength gap, find a complementary member rather than training an existing member.
4. **Define handoff rules** — clarify when Wolf leads and when Bie leads to avoid conflict.
5. **Rotate exposure** — let Wolves occasionally experience Bie work and vice versa to build empathy, but do not confuse primary roles.

### STOP Checkpoint

Before finalizing team composition, verify:
- [ ] Are we selecting for peak strengths rather than absence of weaknesses?
- [ ] Is every critical capability covered by at least one member's strength?
- [ ] Have we explicitly paired Wolf-type and Bie-type roles?
- [ ] Are team members clear on who owns which decisions?
- [ ] Is the team's existence justified by a clear output target?

## Output Format

When activated, provide:
1. A **strength inventory** of the current or proposed team.
2. A **gap-and-complement map** showing missing capabilities and how to fill them.
3. A **Wolf/Bie role assignment** with decision-rights clarity.
4. A **team charter** with output targets and cooperation protocols.
